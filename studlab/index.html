StudLab

StudLab is an iPad app for designing brick models in 3D. It works with the LDraw™ file format and parts library, and has advanced editing features such as part snapping and movable joints. With StudLab you can build a model piece by piece, organize it into instruction steps, and then build it again step by step, just as you would do with a real set. You can export the instructions as a PDF, and the parts list as a CSV file.

Privacy

StudLab collects no data.

  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and no third-party code that does any of these.
  • Models stay where you save them: on your iPad, or in the storage you choose in Files (such as iCloud Drive).
  • Settings are kept on your iPad.

StudLab only goes online when you ask it to:

  • LDraw Official Model Repository (library.ldraw.org), when you open a set from it, to download the set's model and picture.

The services it connects to can see the requests themselves (for example your IP address); what they do with them is covered by their own privacy policies.
